👋 About Us

Built by engineers,
designed for everyone.

Cobbles Tech LLC is a US-based software company. We come from infrastructure and AI backgrounds — we know how to build things that actually hold. "Cobble" is craft, not shortcuts.

Cobbles Tech LLC is founded by engineers who got tired of using apps that were loud, greedy, and just okay. We set out to make software that's quiet, honest, and actually good.

Every app we ship wears a pigeon name. Pigeons are unfairly maligned — smart, resourceful, unapologetically themselves. We like that energy. Our apps are capable without being overwrought, present without being intrusive, and reliable without being boring.

PigeonSnap is our first app. Receipt scanning that doesn't upload your receipts to some server, doesn't require an account, and doesn't come with a subscription.
