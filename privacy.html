4. How Cinnamon uses your information

Cinnamon is an AI agent. To do what you ask, it needs to understand your request, remember relevant context, and sometimes act through services you connect. Specifically:

AI processing

Your requests and the relevant context are sent to the AI model providers we use to generate Cinnamon's responses and plans. We use providers whose terms prohibit them from training on our customers' data. We do not use your content to train our own models.

Memory

Cinnamon remembers information you give it about your household so that it can be useful over time. You can view what it remembers and delete any of it from within the App.

Household groups

If you create or join a household group, information you place in the shared space is visible to other members of that group. Your private space is visible only to you. Group administrators can remove members; removed members lose access to shared data but keep their private space.

Actions on your behalf

When Cinnamon sends a message, makes a booking, or completes a transaction at your direction, the recipient or provider receives the information necessary for that action, exactly as if you had done it yourself. Cinnamon asks for your confirmation before significant actions.

Connected services

We access connected services only within the permissions you grant and only to complete tasks you ask for. We never read or store connected-service data for any other purpose. You can disconnect a service at any time from the App's settings, which revokes our access.

5. What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking platforms.
  • We do not train machine-learning models on your content.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, or usage analytics beyond crash reports unless a specific feature you enable requires it, in which case we ask first.
  • For PigeonSnap, we do not collect your receipts or notes at all: they stay on your device.

6. Data storage and security

Data we hold is stored in encrypted form on secure cloud servers and is encrypted in transit. Access is limited to personnel who need it to operate the service. We follow industry-standard security practices, but no system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs affecting your data, we will notify you promptly in accordance with applicable law.

7. Third parties we share with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the Apps, and only for that purpose:

  • Apple — processes App Store purchases and subscriptions, and Sign in with Apple.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, and any Google services you choose to connect to Cinnamon.
  • AI model providers — process Cinnamon requests to generate responses, under contracts that prohibit training on your data.
  • Cloud hosting and email providers — store data and deliver support email.
  • Services you connect — receive only what is needed to complete the tasks you ask Cinnamon to perform.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — when legally required, and only to the extent required.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Age requirement

Our Apps are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and the Apps are not directed to children. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account or provided us with data, contact us at cobblestech@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export the personal data we hold about you, or to stop processing it. Cinnamon users can also delete their account and all associated data directly from the App. To exercise any right, email cobblestech@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days. Residents of the EU/EEA, UK, California, and other jurisdictions with privacy laws have additional statutory rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with a local data protection authority. We will never discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. International transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where our servers and providers are located. Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Data retention and website cookies

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, as set out in the table in Section 3. When you delete your Cinnamon account, we delete your account data, conversations, and household information within 30 days, except where we must retain records to comply with law. The Site uses only functional, non-tracking cookies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective" date at the top of this page, and where practical we will notify you in the App. Continued use of the Apps after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
